Two Years • A Quiet Note

Gloria,

I wanted to hear your voice, but I thought I should leave this here for you first.

From Christopher — To Gloria

I very much wanted to talk to you on phone, but felt like it would be very awkward, so I decided to use this approach.

I would be lying if I said that you wake up and move away from a two year relationship, unless it meant absolutely nothing, which wasn't the case in our scenario.

I have reached out because I was the one that pulled the trigger to suggest the hardest thing our relationship could face. I read through our heated conversation for a minute, and again and again and again and again, and despite the bare facts that were stated in there, there was still a sense of people that are not tired of each other, but people that are in a life crisis.

These past few days have been sad, and quiet.

“Ahh fuck it, I ain't Shakespeare, but let us work out something.”

After reading this, and you feel the way I feel, the loneliness from the past few days, please call me, I'll be waiting to hear from you.

Our relationship has not been here before, it's a big test. I hope we can mend it.

Christopher
